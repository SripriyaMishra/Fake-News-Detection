How It Works

From raw text to real-time prediction — here's the full pipeline.

1

Data Collection

Curated dataset of labeled real and fake news articles provides the training foundation. Each article is tagged REAL or FAKE by human fact-checkers.

pandas · CSV
2

Text Preprocessing

Raw text is lowercased, stripped of URLs, HTML tags, and special characters. Negation words like "not" and "never" are preserved as they carry important semantic weight.

regex · NLTK
3

Lemmatisation & Stopword Removal

Words are reduced to their base form using WordNet Lemmatizer. Common stopwords are removed while preserving sentiment-bearing negation words.

WordNetLemmatizer · stopwords
4

TF-IDF Vectorization

Text is converted to a 15,000-dimensional numerical vector using Term Frequency–Inverse Document Frequency with unigrams and bigrams. Sublinear TF scaling is applied.

TfidfVectorizer · ngram_range=(1,2)
5

Model Training & Selection

Both PassiveAggressiveClassifier and Logistic Regression are trained and evaluated. The higher-accuracy model is automatically selected and saved with pickle.

PAC · LogisticRegression · pickle
6

Prediction & Confidence Score

The Flask API receives user text, preprocesses it identically to training data, vectorizes, and returns a REAL/FAKE verdict with confidence percentage and category.

Flask · predict_proba · JSON
📖 About

Fighting Misinformation with AI

Understanding the problem and our machine learning solution.

📰

What Is Fake News?

Fake news is deliberately fabricated information designed to deceive readers, spread fear, or manipulate opinions. It often uses emotionally charged language, unverified sources, sensational headlines, and conspiracy framing to appear credible while spreading misinformation.

🤖

How AI Detects It

Machine learning models learn statistical patterns from thousands of verified real and fake articles. Key signals include linguistic style, vocabulary distribution, sentence structure, and presence of sensationalist markers — all captured through TF-IDF feature extraction.

⚖️

Model Limitations

No automated system is 100% accurate. This tool supplements — but does not replace — critical human judgment. Always cross-reference important news with multiple reputable sources, check publication dates, and verify author credentials before sharing.

🌍

Why It Matters

Misinformation spreads six times faster than truthful news on social media. It influences elections, fuels violence, and undermines public health. AI-powered detection tools can help individuals verify information quickly and resist manipulation at scale.
